Important Payment Architecture Distinction:

1. Direct UPI Mode: Uses the administrator's configured UPI VPA below to generate standard upi://pay intents and QR codes. Requires manual administrator reconciliation against bank/UPI statements before an order is marked PAID.

2. Payment Gateway Mode (Razorpay): Uses server-side provider credentials and signed HMAC-SHA256 webhooks for automatic verification. Entering a UPI VPA here does not override gateway merchant settlement accounts.

Interactive Demonstration Scenarios (A – E)

Click any required scenario below to execute and inspect the live state transitions.

Scenario A — Online UPI Payment & Reconciliation

Places an Online UPI order (status PENDING, never falsely PAID), submits UTR reference, and opens Admin Reconciliation to verify receipt and activate the green PAID QR token.

Scenario B — Pay at Canteen & Counter Handover

Places a Pay-at-Canteen order, generates an amber UNPAID QR token with balance due, records counter cash payment (upgrading badge to PAID without breaking the QR), and redeems the token.

Scenario C — Full Preferred Slot & Alternatives

Attempts to book a slot that has reached 0 remaining capacity. Shows the full-slot rejection banner and presents available alternative pickup slots for explicit student selection.

Scenario D — Slot-Aware Optimization & Stochastic DES

Estimates arrival/service rates from observations, runs M/M/c Erlang-C stability checks, executes Integer Counter Optimization across slots, and runs 25-replication DES comparison.

Scenario E — Unfinished Payment & Invalid/Duplicate QR Rejection

Initiates UPI payment without completing it (verifying no PAID token is issued), demonstrates payment-QR vs order-QR rejection, and blocks duplicate token redemption.
